Give me the 3 most significant news stories from the last 24 hours.

Requirements:
- Cover at least 3 different topic areas (e.g. geopolitics, economy, science, sport, technology) — one story each.
- Use 3 different news outlets. Do not take two stories from the same outlet.
- Do NOT use aggregators as your source (no Wikipedia, no news-roundup or "current events portal" pages). Cite the outlet that actually reported the story.
- For each story, give exactly: a one-line headline, one sentence of substance, and the source URL on its own line prefixed with "Source: ".
- Keep it short. Only report stories you actually retrieved. If you cannot find 3, say so and give the ones you have.
